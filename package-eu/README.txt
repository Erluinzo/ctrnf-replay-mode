Replay Mode 0.2.2 BETA - by Onur

Beta: expect bugs, maybe crashes too. Back up your save first.
Bug or low fps? https://github.com/Erluinzo/ctrnf-replay-mode/issues

Records every race (also online) and plays it back as a real race: every
racer drives its recorded line with its own driver, kart, look, items and
hits, and you get the game's own race HUD. Battles are not recorded.

Main menu: REPLAYS (above Quit Game): the list of races, made like the
game's track list, with each race's stats (driver, place and time,
winner, racers, laps, length).
  Up / Down   choose          Cross     play (starts at once)
  Square      keep / don't    Triangle  export
  Circle      back
Any lap count works; a race is recorded for up to 31 minutes.
The newest 50 races are saved; kept ones are never deleted. Every Time
Trial personal best is kept by itself.

While watching:
  L1 / R1            previous / next lap
  L2 / R2            previous / next racer
  Square             pause
  Triangle           HUD style (as in a race)
  D-pad up/down      speed (x0.1 to x4)
  D-pad left/right   rewind / fast forward
  Options            Restart (from the start) or Quit

The HUD shows the watched racer: place, lap and lap times, wumpa, item,
powerslide meter or speedometer. Under it: its name, speed (km/h) and the
replay state. Online, other players' items appear when they used them
(what they held before is not known; older recordings have none, except a
clock). A TNT on a racer's head and a shield's end are recorded too. A replay of an online race passes through the wait before GO in about a
second, so track hazards are where they were.

Files: OCTR folder > user > savedata > 1 > CUSA13795 (EU: CUSA14876) >
RaceReplay. Exports are in RaceReplay > export. To watch a replay from
someone else: they send you a file from their RaceReplay > export folder
(Triangle); put it in RaceReplay (game closed).

Game: CTR Nitro-Fueled 1.21 on the OCTR launcher (v0.8 or newer). This ZIP is for USA
(CUSA13795); the -eu ZIP is for EU (CUSA14876).
